Why Choose Carolina Cleaning Boys?

Eco-Friendly Soft Washing

We use biodegradable soft-washing solutions that safely break down mold, mildew, algae, and grime without harsh pressure. Within about 20 days, the products break down into carbon and water, making them safe for your home and the environment.

Communication Guarantee

When you contact Carolina Cleaning Boys — by phone, text, or email — you can expect a response within two days, often much sooner. If we ever fail to meet that standard, we'll make it right by offering a free service.

Student-Owned & Locally Operated

We're proud to be a small business owned and operated by East Carolina University students. Choosing Carolina Cleaning Boys means supporting local college students who are hardworking, dependable, and invested in the Greenville community.

100% Satisfaction Guaranteed

We don't consider a job complete until both you and our team are fully satisfied with the results. That's why we don't collect payment until the work meets our high standards — and yours.
