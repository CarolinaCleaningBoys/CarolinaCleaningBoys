# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Eastern North Carolina homeowners — primarily in Pitt County and surrounding areas (Greenville, Ayden, Farmville, Raleigh suburbs). They're looking at a stained driveway, green-streaked siding, or clogged gutters and want someone local, affordable, and trustworthy to fix it. Many are near-campus landlords or ECU-area renters prepping for move-out. They find the site through Google Ads or local search, land on a service or location page, and need to feel confident enough to request a quote within 60 seconds.

## Product Purpose

Generate leads for Carolina Cleaning Boys, a student-owned pressure washing and exterior cleaning business in Greenville, NC. The site is the entire sales funnel: it builds trust (real job photos, real reviews, local detail), explains services, and funnels visitors to the quote form or phone number. Success = more quote requests, higher conversion rate, lower bounce rate on service/location pages.

## Brand Personality

Scrappy, trustworthy, local.

- **Scrappy:** Student-owned, bootstrapped, hustle-driven. "Turning Dirt into Degrees" isn't just a tagline — it's the identity. Not a franchise, not a corporation, just college kids who show up and do excellent work.
- **Trustworthy:** Real before/after photos, real Google reviews, real local geography. No stock imagery, no fabricated testimonials. The proof is in the concrete.
- **Local:** Greenville-rooted, ECU-proud. References to specific NC roads, neighborhoods, counties, and climate. This isn't a national template — it's built for people who know what Pitt County humidity does to vinyl siding.

Tone: confident but down-to-earth. Plain language that converts. Specific over generic. "We cleaned 40 driveways in Winterville last summer" beats "We provide exceptional service."

## Anti-references

- **Generic franchise sites** (ServiceMaster, Stanley Steemer, national chains): cookie-cutter templates, stock photos, 1-800 numbers, corporate feel. Carolina Cleaning Boys is the opposite of this.
- **Overly slick agency-designed sites**: too polished, too corporate, loses the student-owned authenticity. The site should feel real and earned, not bought.
- **Cheap DIY / Wix template sites**: clip-art logos, bad fonts, looks thrown together. The site should be well-crafted — scrappy doesn't mean sloppy.
- **AI-generated marketing sites**: beige backgrounds, gradient text, identical card grids, "lorem ipsum energy." The site uses real photos and real copy about real places.

## Design Principles

1. **Proof over promise.** Show the work — before/after photos, real reviews, specific job counts. Every claim should be backed by something visible.
2. **Local specificity wins.** Name the town, the county, the climate condition. Generic copy is invisible; specific copy converts.
3. **Authenticity is the brand.** Student-owned is the differentiator. Don't hide it, don't polish it away — lean into it.
4. **Fast to trust, fast to act.** A visitor should feel confident and find the quote form within one scroll. Don't make them hunt.
5. **Earned, not bought.** The design should feel like quality work from a crew that cares, not a template someone paid for.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG AA compliance.** 4.5:1 minimum contrast for body text, 3:1 for large text. Keyboard navigable, screen-reader friendly.
- **Reduced motion support.** Already implemented via `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` — all animations collapse to instant transitions, decorative elements hidden.
- **Mobile-first.** Most visitors arrive from Google Ads on phones. Touch targets, readable text, and fast load times are non-negotiable.
