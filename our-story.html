What “Turning Dirt into Degrees” Means to Us

Every job we run pays for something real — a semester, a textbook, gas money to get to class. We didn't want student loans to be the only way to pay for school, so we picked up a pressure washer instead.

The motto isn't a line we came up with for a t-shirt. It's what actually happens every time we book a job: dirt off a driveway, money into an education. That's the whole business, in five words.

Why We Hire College Students

We hire fellow ECU students and build the schedule around their classes and practice, not the other way around. Nobody has to choose between a shift and an exam.

Team leads run their own rig and keep 15% of the revenue they bring in — real ownership, not just an hourly check. We're not trying to build a workforce that clocks in and disappears. We're trying to build a place where a student can make real money on their own schedule and learn how to run something along the way.
