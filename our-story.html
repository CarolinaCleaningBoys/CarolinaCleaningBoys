What “Turning Dirt into Degrees” Means to Us

Every job pays for something real, a semester, a textbook, gas, and even rent. We didn't want student loans to be the only way college kids could pay for school, so we picked up a pressure washer instead.

The motto isn't a line we came up with for a t-shirt, it's what actually happens every time we clean a home, wash a driveway, a window, and help a client. That's the whole business in four words.

Where We Come From

Nick and Zach are from Charlotte, North Carolina and brought their exterior cleaning expertise here to Greenville, NC. They earned their engineering degrees from ECU in 2026 and finish their master's in spring 2027, and they apply many of the technical aspects that keep CCB moving!

Sam operated a landscaping business in high school and specializes in business management. He has strong networking skills and brings his great communication to every client.

Student-Athletes First

Nick, Zach, and Sam came to ECU to compete on the university's cross country, indoor and outdoor track teams. They were all successful athletes and competed competitively at conference meets. Unlike many other sports, running demands an extensive time commitment year round, and this has taught them that success requires hard work, but more importantly, smart work.

Strong communication, coordination, and hard work enable us to reach our goals and provide exceptional service to every client!

Why We Hire College Students

We hire college students and build the schedule around their classes and practice, not the other way around. Nobody has to choose between a shift and an exam.

We value growth, which is why we always push our techs to do more than wash houses. Communication, responsibility and time management are just a few of the skills every CCB member picks up here. We're not trying to build a workforce that clocks in and disappears. We're trying to build a place where a student can make real money on their own schedule and learn how to be successful past work.

Paying It Forward

We have big plans here at CCB. Supporting college students matters to us, and so does teaching them skills that hold up well past the job — not just how to clean a house, but how to handle a customer, own a problem and manage their own time.

Next up are internship spots, so students can get real experience in sales, management and logistics while they're still in school. From there we plan to expand into the Raleigh area and beyond as the business grows.

The short version: every job we book is one more opportunity we can hand to another student trying to get through school the same way we did.
