Professional Roof Cleaning Services in Winterville

Carolina Cleaning Boys is a student-run crew from nearby Greenville, and roof cleaning is the one service where technique matters most. In Winterville's humid climate, the shingle and metal roofs in the newer subdivisions off Laurie Ellis Rd and Reedy Branch Rd get streaked with Gloeocapsa magma algae — those dark stains creeping down the north-facing slopes. The critical thing to know: a roof should never be pressure washed. High pressure strips the protective granules off shingles and can force water underneath, so we soft wash roofs only, using low pressure and a cleaning solution that kills the algae at the root. It's the safe, manufacturer-approved way to clear the streaks and add years to your roof, backed by our 100% satisfaction guarantee.

High pressure will strip the protective granules right off asphalt shingles and can force water under them, so we soft wash roofs only — never high pressure. Those dark streaks are Gloeocapsa magma, a blue-green algae that feeds on the limestone filler in asphalt shingles and thrives in humid Eastern NC. It spreads fastest on shaded, north-facing slopes. Our soft wash treatment kills the algae at the root so the streaks are removed, not just rinsed off the surface.
