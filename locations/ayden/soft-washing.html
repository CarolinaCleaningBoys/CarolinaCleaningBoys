Professional Soft Washing Services in Ayden

Carolina Cleaning Boys is student-owned and based just up the road in Greenville, so cleaning homes around Ayden is home turf. Eastern NC humidity is hard on siding — north-facing walls streak black with mildew, and a green algae film creeps across shaded vinyl and painted wood every summer. Instead of blasting it with high pressure, we soft wash: low pressure paired with a cleaning solution that kills the growth at the root so it stays gone longer.
