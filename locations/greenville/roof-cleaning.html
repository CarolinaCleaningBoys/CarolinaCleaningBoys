Professional Roof Cleaning Services in Greenville

Those black streaks running down Greenville roofs are Gloeocapsa magma, a blue-green algae that thrives in Pitt County's humid, subtropical air and feeds on the limestone filler in asphalt shingles. The only right way to remove it is a soft wash — low pressure plus a cleaning solution that kills the algae at the root. High pressure on a roof strips the protective granules off your shingles and can void the warranty, so we never do it, on shingle or metal. From the ranch homes off Charles Blvd to two-story houses in Brook Valley and Cherry Oaks, we clear the streaks, moss, and lichen and leave the roof looking years younger. Started by ECU students right here in Greenville, we quote honestly and treat your roof like our own.
