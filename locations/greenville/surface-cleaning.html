Professional Surface Cleaning Services in Greenville

Greenville sits on Eastern NC's red-clay soil, and that clay has a way of working its way into concrete — leaving driveways off Fire Tower Road and Evans Street a dingy orange-brown that a garden hose won't touch. We clean flat concrete with a surface-cleaner attachment instead of a bare wand: it spins two nozzles under a hood at a fixed height, so the whole slab comes out one even shade with none of the wand streaks you get freehand. Driveways, sidewalks, patios, and pool decks all get the same treatment, and we degrease oil spots and lift the black mildew that builds up on shaded sections.
