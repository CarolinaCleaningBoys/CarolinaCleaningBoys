Data Deletion Request

Last updated: August 13, 2026

If you want us to delete the personal information we hold about you, you can ask and we'll take care of it. This page explains exactly how to make that request, what gets deleted, and how long it takes. It applies whether you contacted us through our website, by text, or through Facebook, Instagram, or Messenger.

1 How to Request Deletion

Email us at CarolinaCleaningBoys@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request."

So we can find your records and be sure we're deleting the right person's information, please include:

  • Your full name
  • The phone number and/or email address you used to contact us
  • The service address, if we've quoted or cleaned a property for you
  • If you contacted us on Facebook or Instagram, the name on that account

You can also call or text us at 919-717-4653 and ask us to delete your information. Email is better simply because it gives us a written record of the request.

2 What We Delete

When we process your request, we remove the personal information we hold about you, including:

  • Your name, phone number, email address, and service address
  • Quotes and estimates we prepared for you
  • Text messages, emails, and Messenger conversations between us
  • Any photos of your property we took or you sent us
  • Notes our team added about your property or job

This covers information held in our customer system and in any connected tools we use to run the business.

3 Facebook, Instagram & Messenger

If you found us through Facebook or Instagram, filled out one of our ad forms, or messaged us on Messenger, we may hold your name, contact details, and the messages you sent us.

To delete that information

Email CarolinaCleaningBoys@gmail.com with the name on your Facebook or Instagram account and let us know you want your data deleted. We'll remove your messages and contact details from our system and confirm once it's done.

Removing our app's access from your Facebook settings stops any further sharing, but it doesn't delete what we already have. To have that removed, send us the request above.

We don't sell or share your information for marketing, and we never have.

4 How Long It Takes

We'll confirm we received your request within a few business days, and complete the deletion within 30 days.

If we can't match your request to a record — usually because the name or number doesn't line up with anything we have — we'll email you back and ask for one more detail rather than guessing.

5 What We May Have to Keep

If we've completed paid work for you, we're required to keep basic financial records of that transaction for tax and accounting purposes. That's typically the invoice amount, the date, and the service address.

We'll tell you if this applies to you. Everything else — your contact details, messages, photos, and notes — is deleted either way, and we won't contact you again.

6 If You Just Want the Texts to Stop

You don't have to delete everything to stop hearing from us.

To stop texts: reply STOP to any message from us. That takes effect immediately and we won't text you again.

For help: reply HELP to any message, or email CarolinaCleaningBoys@gmail.com.

This is often what people actually want. If you'd still like a quote someday, opting out of texts keeps that door open in a way full deletion doesn't.
