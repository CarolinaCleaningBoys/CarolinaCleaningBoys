About the $50 moving fee

If we show up and items are still in the way of the areas we’re cleaning, our technicians can move them for a $50 fee. Whether it applies is entirely at the technician’s discretion, based on how much needs moving — if it’s just a chair or two, we’ll usually move it and think nothing of it. We’d genuinely rather you moved everything yourself, though. It’s your furniture and your belongings, and the last thing we want is to be the ones who break something. Easiest way to skip the fee, and the risk: clear the areas the night before.

How we clean each surface

Not everything gets blasted with high pressure. Most of the house never gets touched by high pressure at all. We match the method to the surface.

House Soft Wash

Soft wash · Low pressure
Pressure level: Low

We clean siding with a soft wash: a cleaning solution at a lower concentration, applied at low pressure and rinsed off. It kills the mold, mildew and algae at the root instead of just knocking the surface green off, so your house stays clean longer and your siding, paint and seals don’t get beat up.

Heads up: A house soft wash doesn’t include the brick around your foundation unless it’s listed on your quote. Foundation brick needs high pressure and different equipment than the soft wash we use on siding. If you want it cleaned, let us know and we’ll add it.

Roof Soft Wash

Soft wash · No pressure
Pressure level: Low

Roofs get the same soft wash method as houses at a higher concentration, with no pressure on the shingles. Those black streaks are a living algae, and this is what actually gets rid of it without damaging your roof.

Driveways, Sidewalks and Patios

Pressure washing · Surface cleaner
Pressure level: High

Concrete is tough, so we clean it with pressure. We use a surface cleaner for an even, streak-free finish across the whole slab, then detail the edges.

Brick

Pressure · Adjusted for age
Pressure level: Medium

Brick in good shape gets cleaned with pressure. If your brick or mortar is older or looking worn, we pretreat it first and then use a softer pressure so we get it clean without eating into the mortar.

Window Cleaning

Pure water · No pressure
Pressure level: Low

Glass is cleaned with pure water, filtered four times so there’s nothing left in it to leave a spot when it dries. No soap, no chemicals. Outside we work from the ground with a water-fed pole, so no ladders lean on your gutters; inside we do it by hand with a mop and squeegee. Frames, tracks and sills get wiped down on every window we clean.

Also available: screens removed, washed and reinstalled, storm windows, glass doors, skylights, hard water spot removal and deep track detail. Interior glass is done on request — just let us know when you book.

Decks and Wood

Wood is the one surface where you get a choice. There are three ways to clean it, and the right one depends on what you want the deck to look like when we’re done.

Refresh

Intensity: Gentle

Intensity: Gentle · Best for: a solid general clean on a budget

A good all-around clean that removes the dirt, mildew and green. The wood can look a little duller afterward than with the other two options. This is usually the most affordable choice.

Cleaner: sodium hypochlorite (the same solution we use on houses and roofs)

Restore

Intensity: Moderate

Intensity: Moderate · Best for: bringing back the natural wood color

A softer, less aggressive wood cleaner that still brings back the original warm wood tone. A great middle ground if you want the deck to look like wood again without stripping it down.

Cleaner: sodium percarbonate, followed by oxalic acid

Strip & Prep

Intensity: Heavy

Intensity: Heavy · Best for: getting ready to stain or paint

Our most aggressive wood clean. It strips everything off the surface, old stain included, and leaves the wood ready for fresh stain or paint. It restores both the color and the cleanliness of the deck.

Cleaner: sodium metasilicate, followed by oxalic acid

Not sure which one you need? Text us a photo of your deck and we’ll tell you.

Protecting Your Plants

Every time we clean, whether it’s the house, roof, driveway, brick or deck, we soak your plants and landscaping with water before we start and rinse them again when we’re done. That keeps our cleaning solution from sitting on them.

It works. We’ve rarely had any issues. But we want to be upfront: any time cleaning solution is used near landscaping, there’s some risk that a plant reacts poorly. We take every precaution we can, and we’d rather you hear that from us now than be surprised later.

A little rain doesn’t stop us. We’re getting everything wet anyway, so light rain usually doesn’t affect the clean.

Storms or heavy rain are a different story. If it’s storming or pouring, we’ll text you to let you know we need to reschedule and find the next time that works for you. That call is up to us, and there’s no charge when we reschedule for weather.

If you don’t get a text from us canceling, assume we’re still coming.

The walk-around

Before we leave, we want to walk the property with you. It’s how we catch anything we missed while we’re still there with all the equipment set up.

  • If you’re home, come on out and we’ll walk around together.
  • If you’re not home, we’ll send you photos, or hop on a FaceTime or video call, whichever you prefer. We ask that you take a look within 24 to 48 hours.

Once you’ve done the walk-around and given us the thumbs up, the job is complete. That’s why we ask you to look closely. We’d much rather fix something on the spot than find out about it a week later, and after sign-off we aren’t able to send a technician back out.

Our 100% satisfaction guarantee

If something isn’t right during the walk-around, we make it right before the job is closed out. You don’t pay until you’re happy with it. That’s how we’ve earned 200+ five-star reviews, and we’re not about to change it now.
