Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Soft washing uses low-pressure water combined with a specialized cleaning solution to safely clean delicate surfaces. Unlike pressure washing, which relies on high pressure, soft washing kills mold, mildew, and algae at the root without risking damage to siding, roofs, or painted surfaces. The result is a longer lasting, better looking clean compared to standard pressure washing.

Yes! Soft washing is the safest and most effective method for cleaning vinyl siding. The low pressure won't crack, dent, or force water behind the siding like high-pressure washing can. Our cleaning solutions remove dirt, mold, and mildew without damaging your home or the environment!

House washing typically costs between $200-$500 depending on the size of your home and level of buildup. We provide free, no-obligation quotes so you'll know the exact price before we start.

Yes! Our soft washing solutions are specifically designed to kill mold, mildew, and algae at the source. The chemicals break down within about 20 days into just carbon and water, making them safe for your plants, pets, and family.
