Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Soft washing uses low-pressure water combined with specialized biodegradable cleaning solutions to safely clean delicate surfaces. Unlike pressure washing which relies on high pressure, soft washing kills mold, mildew, and algae at the root without risking damage to siding, roofs, or painted surfaces.

Yes! Soft washing is the safest and most effective method for cleaning vinyl siding. The low pressure won't crack, dent, or force water behind the siding like high-pressure washing can. Our eco-friendly solutions remove dirt, mold, and mildew without damaging your home.

House washing typically costs between $200-$500 depending on the size of your home and level of buildup. We provide free, no-obligation quotes so you'll know the exact price before we start.
